About the doctor

Dr.Gopi

Dr. Gopi K.S, BSc, BHMS, MD, Former Professor at the Government Homoeopathic Medical College, Kozhikode, Kerala, India, has over four decades of teaching and research experience in the field of Homoeopathy. He is currently serving as the Director and Chief Consultant at Dr. Gopi’s Clinic, a homeopathic multispeciality clinic. He has published numerous research papers in both national and international journals.

Books published : Encyclopaedia of medicinal plants used in Homoeopathy 10 vols, Homoeopathy a complete family health guide, Easy Prescriber, Herbarium, A text book of Homoeopathic pharmacy, Diseases of skin with Homoeopathic management, Mother tincture and low potency prescription, Homoeopathy psychiatry, Cosmetic medicines in Homoeopathy, Homoeopathy for Baby and child, Sports medicines in Homoeopathy, Cure kidney stones with Homoeopathy, Cure Dengue fever, Chikungunya and Malaria with Homoeopathy, Materia Medica 2 vols, Control hypertension without Medicines, Sexual life- problems and solutions.

Dr. Gopi's Clinic offers holistic homeopathic care, combining decades of expertise with modern treatments for comprehensive patient well-being.
